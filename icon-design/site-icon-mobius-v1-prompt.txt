实际生成提示词：
Edit the supplied existing website icon into one polished logo concept. Use case: precise-object-edit; asset type: app/website brand icon design preview. Image 1 is the edit target, not an unrelated style reference. Preserve its black/very dark charcoal background, vivid lime green and warm white colors, chunky rounded ribbon shapes, balanced compact central composition, and absence of text. The key change: connect the two green sections into ONE continuous CLOSED green ribbon loop, and connect the two white sections into ONE continuous CLOSED white ribbon loop. These two complete ribbon loops interweave in the central four-lobed emblem, each with a clearly visible gentle half-twist suggesting a Möbius strip. No loose ends, no gaps, no four disconnected pieces. The silhouette can be refined to make the closed continuity readable; keep the spirit of the original symmetrical compact green/white mark. Make the ribbons broad, soft and cute/chibi, with clean vector-like solid color regions and subtle, elegant smooth gradients: lime to leaf green within the green ribbon, warm white to a soft cool gray within the white ribbon. Use a few crisp occlusion transitions to distinguish which band passes above or below at crossings; modest stylized depth, not photorealistic. Keep the original dark rounded-square app-icon backdrop and a comfortable margin. One icon only, centered in a square composition. No typography, no labels, no mockup, no extra objects, no realistic materials, no metallic chrome, no glossy plastic glare, no rainbow gradient, no thin outlines, no ordinary chain links. The result should feel premium yet playful and simple, legible at small icon size.
